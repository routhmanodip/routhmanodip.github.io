Welcome

Manodip Routh

Postdoctoral Research Fellow · Department of Applied Physics, The University of Tokyo, Japan

I am Manodip Routh, a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Applied Physics at the University of Tokyo, Japan.

My research interests lie in theoretical and computational condensed matter physics, with a particular emphasis on low-dimensional, strongly correlated quantum systems where competing or frustrated interactions play a central role. To investigate these systems, I use numerical methods such as exact diagonalization (ED) and the density matrix renormalization group (DMRG).

Research

My broader research interests lie in the theoretical and computational study of frustrated spin systems in low dimension. I am particularly interested in the topics below:

  • Novel quantum phases in frustrated spin systems
  • Quantum spin liquids, Kitaev model
  • Low-energy effective spin models for real materials (transition-metal compounds, rare-earth compounds)
  • Density matrix renormalization group (DMRG), tensor network methods
